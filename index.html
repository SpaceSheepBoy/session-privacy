中文版

概述

「一座 Session」是一款专注于持咒计数与禅修定时的修行辅助工具。我们尊重并保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

数据收集

我们的应用仅在您的设备本地存储以下信息：

  • 您创建的咒语（名称、每日目标、累计计数）
  • 禅修记录（时长、发愿、回向）
  • 修行历史与统计数据
  • 应用设置偏好（主题、音效、震动、语言等）

数据存储

所有数据仅存储在您的设备本地。我们不会将任何个人数据上传到服务器或与第三方共享。

Apple Watch 同步

如果您使用 Apple Watch 配套应用，计数数据会通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 框架在您的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间同步。此同步完全在您的设备之间进行，不经过任何外部服务器。

导出与备份

应用提供数据导出功能，允许您将修行记录导出为文件。导出的文件存储在您选择的位置，我们不会访问或上传这些文件。

第三方服务

本应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪服务。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

隐私政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在此页面发布，并更新「最后更新」日期。

English Version

Overview

"Session" is a mindful mantra counting and meditation timer app. We respect and protect your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Data Collection

Our app only stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Mantras you create (name, daily goal, accumulated count)
  • Meditation records (duration, intention, dedication)
  • Practice history and statistics
  • App preferences (theme, sound, haptics, language, etc.)

Data Storage

All data is stored only on your device. We do NOT upload any personal data to servers or share with third parties.

Apple Watch Sync

If you use the Apple Watch companion app, count data is synced between your iPhone and Apple Watch via Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This sync occurs entirely between your devices and does not go through any external servers.

Export & Backup

The app provides data export functionality, allowing you to export your practice records as files. Exported files are stored at a location of your choice, and we do not access or upload these files.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us
