中文版

核心承诺：「一座持咒」不接入广告、第三方分析或跨应用追踪。修行数据默认保存在本机；如果您开启 iCloud 同步，数据会通过 Apple CloudKit 保存在您自己的 iCloud 账户中。

概述

「一座持咒」是一款专注于持咒计数、日课念诵与禅修定时的修行辅助工具。我们尊重并保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明应用如何存储、同步和保护您的信息。

我们收集什么

我们不运营用户账户系统，不接入广告 SDK，不使用第三方分析服务，也不会把您的修行数据上传到 Kila Labs 自有服务器。

应用会在设备本地保存您主动创建或产生的内容，例如咒语、发愿、计数记录、禅修记录、日课设置、主题与声音偏好。

数据存储与 iCloud 同步

  • 默认情况下，数据保存在您的 iPhone、iPad 或 Apple Watch 本地。
  • 如果您开启 iCloud 同步，修行数据会通过 Apple CloudKit 存储在您的个人 iCloud 账户中，用于在自己的 Apple 设备之间同步。
  • Kila Labs 不运营可读取这些数据的服务器，也不会将这些数据出售或共享给广告网络。
  • 关闭 iCloud 同步后，应用会停止主动向 iCloud 同步新的更改。

Apple Watch、Widget 与 Live Activities

Apple Watch App、桌面小组件和实时活动会通过 Apple 平台能力读取或显示当前计数、计时和会话状态。这些信息用于让您在锁屏、桌面或手表上继续修行，不用于追踪。

权限说明

  • 照片：仅在您保存分享卡片到相册，或选择文件/图片导入时使用。
  • 通知：仅在您启用殊胜日提醒时，用于本地提醒。
  • 音频：用于播放计数音效、禅修提示音和随诵音频。
  • iCloud：仅在您启用 iCloud 同步时，用于跨设备同步修行数据。

第三方服务

本应用不使用第三方广告、分析或追踪服务。应用中可能提供外部来源链接，打开后将由对应网站自己的隐私政策约束。

导出、备份与分享

应用提供数据导出和分享卡片功能。导出的文件或分享图片只会保存到您选择的位置，或通过系统分享面板交给您选择的目标应用。

English Version

Our promise: Yizuo Mantra does not include ads, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. Practice data is stored locally by default. If you enable iCloud Sync, your data is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple CloudKit.

Overview

Yizuo Mantra is a practice companion for mantra counting, daily recitation, and meditation timing. We respect and protect your privacy. This policy explains how the app stores, syncs, and protects your information.

What We Collect

We do not operate user accounts, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or Kila Labs servers that receive your practice data.

The app stores the content you create or generate on your device, such as mantras, vows, count records, meditation sessions, daily-practice settings, theme preferences, and sound preferences.

Data Storage and iCloud Sync

  • By default, data is stored locally on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch.
  • If you enable iCloud Sync, practice data is stored in your personal iCloud account through Apple CloudKit so it can sync across your own Apple devices.
  • Kila Labs does not operate a server that can read this data, and we do not sell or share this data with advertising networks.
  • After iCloud Sync is turned off, the app stops actively syncing new changes to iCloud.

Apple Watch, Widgets, and Live Activities

The Apple Watch app, Home Screen widget, and Live Activities use Apple platform features to read or display your current count, timer, and session state. This is used to continue practice from the Lock Screen, Home Screen, or watch, not for tracking.

Permissions

  • Photos: Used only when you save a share card to Photos or choose a file/image for import.
  • Notifications: Used only for local holy-day reminders if you enable them.
  • Audio: Used to play counting sounds, meditation bells, and recitation audio.
  • iCloud: Used only when you enable iCloud Sync for cross-device practice-data sync.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. External source links may open websites governed by their own privacy policies.

Export, Backup, and Sharing

The app provides data export and share-card features. Exported files and share images are saved only to a location you choose or passed to apps you select through the system share sheet.

Support and Contact

If you have trouble using the app, or want to report an issue with mantras, daily practice, sync, Apple Watch, widgets, or share cards, please contact us by email. Please include your device model, OS version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and screenshots if available.

Email: lain0911@126.com

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes, we will update this page and revise the date at the top.
